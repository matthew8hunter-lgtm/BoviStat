Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BoviStat (“BoviStat”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use BoviStat.ca (the “Site”).

1) Information we collect

  • Contact submissions: If you use our contact form, we collect the information you provide (name, email, message).
  • Usage data: We may collect basic technical information (e.g., browser type, device type, pages visited) for security and performance.
  • Results / rankings data: If results are submitted (by you or a third party), we may store show-related information such as exhibitor name, animal name/ID, breed, class/division, placement, show name/date, and location.

2) How we use information

  • To operate and improve the Site and features (leaderboards, profiles, statistics).
  • To respond to inquiries and support requests.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues.
  • To maintain the accuracy and integrity of show results and rankings.

3) Cookies

We may use cookies or similar technologies to keep the Site functioning and to understand traffic. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy accordingly.

4) Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With service providers that help us run the Site (hosting, forms, analytics) only as needed.
  • If required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5) Public information on the Site

Leaderboards, results, and profiles are intended to be public. If you believe information is inaccurate or should be removed, contact us and we will review the request.

6) Data retention

We retain data as long as needed to operate the Site, maintain historical results, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

7) Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data, but no system is 100% secure. Please use caution when submitting sensitive information.

8) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Effective date” above reflects the latest version.

9) Contact

Questions or requests? Contact us at BoviStat Contact.
